Example 7: Sound Flash Periphery

Instructions

  1. Press Run. Look at the white cross in the middle.
  2. Flashes alternate left and right at the edges of your vision. Click sounds play with each flash.
  3. Every other flash includes a double click — when the double click plays, does that flash also seem to flicker twice?
  4. Compare with sound off, and play with the sliders to find your own thresholds.
