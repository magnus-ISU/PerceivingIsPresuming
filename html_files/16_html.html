Example 16: Two Grays

Instructions

  1. Look at the image — the upper square A and the lower square B appear to be very different shades of gray.
  2. Click and drag the small gray rectangle in the upper-left over the A and B squares.
  3. The rectangle is exactly the same shade of gray as both A and B — your brain is correcting for the apparent lighting context.
  4. Use Show Connection to draw a uniform bridge between the two squares, and Show Outline to add a border that breaks the lightness-constancy effect.
