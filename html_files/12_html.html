Example 12: Ames Room

Instructions

  1. Watch the video to see two people of similar height appear dramatically different in size.
  2. The Ames Room is a forced-perspective interior — the back wall is actually a trapezoid, and one corner is much closer to the camera than it appears.
  3. Even after learning how the trick works, the size illusion persists.
  4. Drag the seek slider below the video to jump to any point.
