Example 1: Hollow Face

Instructions

  1. In auto-play mode, click Run to watch the mask rotate. Adjust the speed slider to see whether playback speed changes your experience.
  2. Notice that the painted (concave) side of the mask often looks convex, even though it's facing away.
  3. Switch to manual mode to step through the rotation one frame at a time. Tap a (or the matching button) to advance, d to reverse.
  4. Identify the frame where the mask first switches from concave to convex — that's where your visual prior takes over.
