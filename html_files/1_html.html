Example 1: Hollow Face

Instructions

  1. Click Run to watch the mask rotate continuously. Click again to pause.
  2. Adjust the Speed slider to see whether playback speed changes your experience of the illusion.
  3. While paused, use the a / d keys (or the step buttons) to advance and reverse one frame at a time.
  4. Notice the painted (concave) side often looks convex even though it's facing away. Find the precise frame where your perception flips.
