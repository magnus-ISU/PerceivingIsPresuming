Example 2: That Fat Bat (McGurk Illusion)

Instructions

  1. Use the speaker selector to choose Meredith, David, or Rhea.
  2. Press Run — the video and audio loop together.
  3. Listen for what you hear. Try Change Audio to swap between the two possible recordings.
  4. Use Show Words to see the actual words on the audio track, and Show Video to hide the face and listen alone.
  5. Hit Restart if the audio drifts out of sync with the video.
