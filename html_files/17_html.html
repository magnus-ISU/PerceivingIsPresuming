Example 17: Moon Walk

The original demonstration for this example was a YouTube clip that has since been removed due to a copyright claim.

The illusion involves a person seemingly walking forward and backward depending on whether you interpret their motion as gliding feet or stepping feet — your brain's prior about walking dominates the raw motion signal.

Searching YouTube for "moonwalk illusion perception" will turn up similar demonstrations.

Instructions

  1. When a walking video is available, watch for the moment your interpretation of the direction of motion flips.
  2. The classic version: a person appears to glide as if moonwalking, then suddenly looks like they're walking normally — same pixels, different prior.
