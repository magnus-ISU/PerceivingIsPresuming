Example 11: Reporter

LISTEN CLOSELY NOW

 

Instructions

  1. Press Run and listen to the reporter — the words may sound like gibberish.
  2. Toggle Show Words to see a possible interpretation of what's being said.
  3. Use the text rotator to switch between two competing interpretations and notice how each one makes the audio fit.
  4. When the on-screen "LISTEN CLOSELY NOW" banner appears, focus your hearing — that's the key segment.
