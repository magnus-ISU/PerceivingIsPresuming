Example 19: Hollow Face 2

Instructions

  1. Click anywhere on the page so it has keyboard focus.
  2. Hold the a key (or the matching button) to advance the rotation forward until the painted side first appears.
  3. Tap a to advance one frame at a time, stopping when the mask first looks convex.
  4. Tap d (or the back button) to reverse one frame at a time until it looks concave again.
  5. Note the frame number where the mask first looks convex — that's where your visual prior takes over.
