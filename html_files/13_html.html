Example 13: Ames Room Revisited

Instructions

  1. Rotate the room with the slider to see how the same scene viewed from different angles produces very different size impressions.
  2. Notice how the back wall, which appears to be a normal rectangular wall, is actually slanted away from the viewer.
  3. The figures appear to grow and shrink as they move across the room, even though they remain the same physical size.
