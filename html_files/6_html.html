Example 6: Sound Flash Center

Instructions

  1. Press Run. A circle flashes at the center while a click sound plays.
  2. Every other flash includes a double click — does the flash seem to flash twice when two clicks play?
  3. Try turning Sound off to see the flash without audio, and back on to confirm the difference.
  4. Use the sliders to change the flash's size, color, transparency, timing, and the gap before the second click.
